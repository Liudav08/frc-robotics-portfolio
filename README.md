# FRC Robotics - Team Hot Rods

## Overview

Contributed to the design, fabrication, and integration of FRC competition
robots as a member of the Electrical and Mechanical teams.

My work included electrical system design, robot wiring, CAD, CNC machining,
woodworking, mechanical fabrication, and integration of electrical and
mechanical systems.

## My Contributions

### Electrical

- Designed and assembled robot electrical systems
- Produced electrical wiring/layout diagrams
- Wired motors, sensors, power distribution, and control electronics
- Assisted with electrical troubleshooting and system integration

### Mechanical

- Designed mechanical components and assemblies using CAD
- Fabricated components using CNC machining and woodworking
- Assisted with drivetrain and mechanism construction
- Integrated mechanical assemblies with electrical systems
